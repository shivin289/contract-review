CONSULTING AGREEMENT

This Consulting Agreement is entered into between Lumen Studio LLP ("Consultant") and Orchard Foods plc ("Customer").

1. Commencement and Duration
The engagement commences on 15 June 2026 and ends on 14 December 2026.

2. Fees
The Customer shall pay the Consultant GBP 8,000 on completion of the engagement. Payment is due within fourteen (14) days of invoice.

3. Termination
Either party may end this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to remedy the breach within ten (10) days.

4. Confidentiality
The Consultant shall treat all non-public information of the Customer as confidential and use it only for the engagement.

5. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
